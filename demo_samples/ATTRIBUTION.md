# Demo Sonar Samples Attribution & Licensing

This directory contains authentic side-scan sonar and marine acoustic survey imagery used for testing, validation, and demonstrations in the GhostNet project. None of these images are synthetic or AI-fabricated; all are sourced directly from verified public scientific repositories (USGS, NOAA, and affiliated academic research centers).

> [!IMPORTANT]
